TAG

[taeg]

noun
1. a label attached to someone or something for the purpose of identification or to give other information.
"he gave his pet a collar with a metal name tag"
Synonyms: label, ticket, badge, mark, marker, tab, tally, sticker, docket, stub, chilt, chitt, counterfoi, flag, stamp
"a price tag"
2. a small piece or part that is attached to a main body.
verb
1. attach a label to.
"mothers suspects that their babies had been wrongly tagged during an alarm at the hospital"
Synonyms: label, attach tags to, put a label on, mark, ticket, earmark, identify, docket, flag, indicate
"the bottles were lagged with colour-coded labels"
2. add to something, especially, as ab afterthought or with no real connection
"she meant to tag her question on at the end of her remarks"
Synonyms: add, tack, join, attach, append, affix, annex
"their heads so large that the rest of their bodies seemed tagged on as an afterthought"
